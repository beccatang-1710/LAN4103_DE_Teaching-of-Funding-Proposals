Follow the 4 Steps to Write Your 25% CA

Step 1: Writing Organisation Information (Unit 2)

第一步：撰写机构资料（单元二）

For writing Organisation information, identify key components on the homepage and use the following grammatical structures:

撰写机构资料时，请从主页中识别关键信息，并运用以下语法结构：
  • Structure 1: [past participle, "-ed"] + in + Establishment/Founding/Formation year
  • 结构一：[过去分词] + in + 成立/创办年份
  • Structure 2: Organization name + is a + [business nature]
  • 结构二：机构名称 + is a + [业务性质]
  • Structure 3: specialising in / dedicated to / committed to / devoted to / focusing on + [action / area]
  • 结构三：specialising in / dedicated to / committed to / devoted to / focusing on + [行动/领域]
  • Structure 4: Additionally, / Also, + we organize / run / conduct / provide / offer + organization's mission / services / work
  • 结构四：Additionally / Also + we organize / run / conduct / provide / offer + 机构使命/服务
  • Structure 5: [to + verb] + purpose of the mission / services / work
  • 结构五：[to + 动词] + 使命/服务的目的

Examples / 范例

第二步：撰写项目详情（单元三）

For writing Project details (Implementation + Amount of funding requested), identify duration and action of each phase on Notes of meeting.

撰写项目详情（执行计划 + 申请资助金额）时，请从会议记录中识别各阶段的时间及行动。

a. Implementation Formula / 执行计划公式

  • The project will be completed in three stages/phases, each lasting XXX months/weeks.
  • 项目将分三个阶段进行，每阶段为期XXX个月/周。
  • In phase 1, we will [verb]..., in phase 2, we will [verb]...
  • 在第一阶段，我们将……；在第二阶段，我们将……
  • In phase 3, / In the final phase, ...
  • 在第三阶段 / 在最后阶段，……

b. Amount of Funding Requested Formula / 申请资助金额公式

  • It is estimated that a total of $xxxxx will be required for the project. For details of the breakdown, please refer to the attachment.
  • 项目预计所需资金总额为$xxxxx。详情请参阅附件。
  • (Alternative) The project is estimated to cost around $xxxxx. The details of the breakdown are attached for your information.
  • （替代写法）项目预计成本约为$xxxxx。费用明细已附上供参考。

Examples / 范例

Example 1 — Film / Drama Project:

a. Implementation: The project will be completed in three phases, each lasting two months. In phase 1, we will finalise the screenplay and cast suitable actors.

范例一 — 电影/戏剧项目：
a. 执行计划：项目将分三个阶段进行，每阶段为期两个月。在第一阶段，我们将敲定剧本并挑选合适的演员。

In phase 2, necessary filming permits will be obtained before scheduling rehearsals. Filming will also be conducted to capture the scripted scenes.

在第二阶段，将先取得所需的拍摄许可证，再安排排练，并进行拍摄以捕捉剧本中的场景。

In the final phase, we will edit the footage and test the submission entry on multiple devices to ensure technical compatibility.

在最后阶段，我们将剪辑影片，并在多个设备上测试提交条目，以确保技术兼容性。

b. Amount of funding requested: It is estimated that a total of $1,000,000 will be required for the project. For details of the breakdown, please refer to the attachment.

b. 申请资助金额：项目预计所需资金总额为1,000,000港元。详情请参阅附件。

Example 2 — Neon Art Exhibition:

a. Implementation: The project will be executed in three stages, each spanning three weeks. In stage 1, we will send invitations to local neon artists and co-create artworks with them.

范例二 — 霓虹灯艺术展览：
a. 执行计划：项目将分三个阶段进行，每阶段为期三周。在第一阶段，我们将向本地霓虹灯艺术家发出邀请，并与他们共同创作艺术品。

In stage 2, the exhibition will be installed at a public venue. Interactive workshops will also be incorporated.

在第二阶段，展览将在公共场所设置，并加入互动工作坊。

In the final stage, we will conduct random interviews with visitors and collect their feedback to evaluate the project's impact for future development.

在最后阶段，我们将随机访问参观者并收集反馈，以评估项目对未来发展的影响。

b. Amount of funding requested: The project is estimated to cost around $400,000. The details of the breakdown are attached for your information.

b. 申请资助金额：项目预计成本约为400,000港元。费用明细已附上供参考。

Step 3: Writing Justification for Funding (Unit 4)

第三步：撰写资助理由（单元四）

For writing Justification, identify 3 benefits on your handwritten notes. Use the following vocabulary and grammatical structures:

撰写资助理由时，请从手写笔记中识别三项益处，并运用以下词汇和语法结构：
  • Benefit 1: The project will + provide / offer / feature / give + [Benefit 1]
  • 益处一：项目将 + 提供/展示/带来 + [益处一]
  • Elaboration: verb+ing (e.g. strengthening / encouraging) + [result / elaboration]
  • 阐述：动词+ing（如加强/促进）+ [结果/阐述]
  • Benefit 2: Additionally, / Moreover, / In addition, it / the project will + [Benefit 2]
  • 益处二：此外 / 而且 / 与此同时，该项目将 + [益处二]
  • Elaboration: which + [result / elaboration]
  • 阐述：which + [结果/阐述]
  • Alignment with Funder: Furthermore, / Also, this project's aim closely aligns with / is in line with the mission and values of [funder].
  • 与资助方的契合：再者，本项目的目标与[资助方]的使命及价值观高度契合。

Examples / 范例

Example 1 (Creative Industry): The competition will provide a platform for our emerging creators to showcase their work, strengthening the industry's creative pipeline.

范例一（创意产业）：比赛将为新晋创作者提供展示作品的平台，加强业界的创意储备。

In addition, the funding will enable us to secure essential production resources, which helps to ensure high-quality post-production work.

此外，资助将使我们能够获取必要的制作资源，有助于确保高质量的后期制作工作。

Furthermore, the aim of the project closely aligns with the mission of the Cultural and Creative Industries Development Agency. It will contribute greatly to the sustainable growth of Hong Kong's cultural and creative industries.

再者，本项目的目标与创意香港的使命高度契合，将大大促进香港文化创意产业的可持续发展。

Example 2 (Neon Art & Heritage): The neon exhibition will highlight the craftsmanship and cultural significance of neon art, encouraging the public to rediscover this unique visual heritage.

范例二（霓虹灯艺术与文化遗产）：霓虹灯展览将突出霓虹灯艺术的工艺及文化意义，鼓励公众重新认识这一独特的视觉遗产。

Local artists will also have a platform to experiment with light-based media, which will foster creative exchange.

本地艺术家亦将获得一个试验光媒体的平台，促进创意交流。

Moreover, the project's aim is in line with the mission and values of the CreateSmart Initiative, resulting in promoting design appreciation and cultural preservation in the community.

此外，本项目的目标与「创意智优计划」的使命及价值观一致，有助促进社区对设计的欣赏及文化保育。

Step 4: Writing Supplementary Information (Unit 5)

第四步：撰写补充资料（单元五）

For writing Supplementary information, identify the possible concern and solution and a concluding remark on your handwritten notes.

撰写补充资料时，请从手写笔记中识别可能的关注事项、解决方案及结语。
  • 1. Acknowledging Concern: We are aware that / We acknowledge / We understand that there might be concerns about ... [concern]
  • 1. 承认关注事项：我们明白 / 我们理解可能会有……的顾虑
  • 2. Proposing Solution: However, we will ensure that... / However, please rest assured that we will... [solution]
  • 2. 提出解决方案：然而，我们将确保…… / 请放心，我们将……
  • 3. Concluding Remark: We are confident that the project / [verb-ing phrase], which [project aim], will [summary of benefits / positive outcome].
  • 3. 结语：我们坚信，该项目 / [动词+ing短语]，[项目目标]，将[益处/正面成果的总结]。

Examples / 范例

Example 1 — Esports Tournament (Technical Server Lag):

We are aware that there might be concerns about technical issues, such as server lag during the tournament. However, please rest assured that we will secure high-performance servers and conduct pre-event stress tests so that a smooth experience for all participants will be guaranteed.

范例一 — 电竞比赛（服务器延迟技术问题）：
我们明白可能会有技术问题的顾虑，例如比赛期间的服务器延迟。请放心，我们将确保使用高性能服务器，并在活动前进行压力测试，以保证所有参与者享有流畅的体验。

We are confident that organising the esports tournament, which showcases the outstanding performance of local gamers, will foster a positive perception of competitive gaming among the public, gaining more support from society.

我们坚信，举办是次电竞比赛，展示本地电竞选手的出色表现，将促进公众对竞技游戏的正面看法，赢得社会更多支持。

Example 2 — EV Charging Station Network (Equipment Safety):

We understand that there might be concerns about the safety of the charging stations. However, we will ensure regular check-ups and maintenance of the charging equipment so that the risk of malfunctions can be minimized.

范例二 — 电动车充电站网络（设备安全）：
我们理解可能会对充电站的安全性有所顾虑。然而，我们将确保定期检查及保养充电设备，以尽量减低故障风险。

We are confident that installing the EV charging station network, which promotes sustainable transportation and a greener future, will have a significant and far-reaching impact on environmental conservation, promoting the adoption of EVs in the community.

我们坚信，安装电动车充电网络，推动可持续交通及更绿色的未来，将对环保产生深远影响，促进社区采用电动车。

Example 3 — Neon Installation Exhibition (Electrical Safety):

We acknowledge that there might be concerns about the safety of neon installations. However, we are committed to ensuring that all artworks comply with electrical safety standards and undergo professional inspection before being displayed in order to minimise risks.

范例三 — 霓虹灯装置展览（电力安全）：
我们承认可能会对霓虹灯装置的安全性有所顾虑。然而，我们致力于确保所有艺术品符合电力安全标准，并在展出前进行专业检查，以尽量减低风险。

We are confident that the neon exhibition, which supports local creative talent, will generate meaningful cultural impact and inspire interest in preserving the city's design legacy.

我们坚信，支持本地创意人才的霓虹灯展览，将产生有意义的文化影响，并激发人们保存城市设计遗产的兴趣。
