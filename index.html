Evaluation Report on 'Old Clothes; New Looks' Project

「旧衣；新貌」项目评估报告

1. Introduction

The trend towards sustainable fashion has gained increasing attention in recent years, particularly among younger generations. However, many teenagers who lack upcycling skills struggle to transform old clothes into trendy designs. This mini-project, 'Old Clothes; New Looks', aims to enhance teenagers' upcycling techniques and inspire them to create fashionable outfits through hands-on workshops. This report aims to discuss the effectiveness of the project and give recommendations for the way forward.

近年来，可持续时尚的趋势日益受到关注，尤其是在年轻一代中。然而，许多缺乏升级改造技能的青少年难以将旧衣服改造成时尚设计。这个名为「旧衣；新貌」的小项目旨在通过实践工作坊提升青少年的升级改造技术，并激励他们创造时尚服装。本报告旨在讨论项目的有效性，并为未来方向提出建议。

2. Methodology

2.1 Participants: Two sustainable fashion experts were recruited as instructors, while 20 teenagers from local community centres were invited to participate in the project.

2.1 参与者：聘请了两名可持续时尚专家作为导师，并邀请了来自本地社区中心的20名青少年参与项目。

2.2 Implementation: From October to November 202-, four workshops were conducted by the instructors, during which they demonstrated how to repurpose old clothes, apply sewing techniques, and add decorative elements. Hands-on practice was also incorporated to allow participants to experiment with their own designs.

2.2 实施：从十月到十一月202-年，导师共举办了四次工作坊，演示如何改造旧衣服、运用缝纫技术及添加装饰元素。工作坊亦设有实践环节，让参与者尝试自己的设计。

2.3 Evaluation: To collect data for evaluation, the participants had been invited to take part in a pre-project survey in September 202- about their clothing upcycling habits. In December 202-, a post-project survey was administered and focus group interviews were arranged for participants to share their experiences.

2.3 评估：为收集评估数据，参与者于202-年九月参加了关于其旧衣改造习惯的项目前调查。202-年十二月进行了项目后调查，并安排了焦点小组访谈，让参与者分享他们的经验。

3. Findings

More than half of the respondents repurposed their old clothes regularly, which was a significant improvement from only 10% before the project. Almost one-third of them did so occasionally, compared to just 5% prior to the project.

超过半数受访者定期改造旧衣服，较项目前仅10%有显著改善。近三分之一受访者偶尔进行改造，而项目前仅为5%。

Moreover, the number of respondents who reported being 'quite confident' in creatively redesigning second-hand clothes into stylish outfits rose sharply from 40% to 80% after the project, while those reporting 'no confidence' fell dramatically from 60% to 0%.

此外，表示「颇有信心」将二手衣物创意改造成时尚服装的受访者比例从40%急剧上升至80%，而表示「没有信心」的比例则从60%大幅下降至0%。

It was also mentioned that the hands-on upcycling activities were engaging and enjoyable. Nevertheless, some participants expressed concerns about forgetting the sewing techniques over time without ongoing support. Furthermore, a few of them found it quite difficult to source quality second-hand clothes.

参与者亦提到，实践性的升级改造活动引人入胜且令人愉快。然而，部分参与者表达了对在缺乏持续支持下会逐渐忘记缝纫技术的担忧。此外，少数参与者表示难以找到优质的二手衣物。

4. Discussion

The findings indicate that the four workshops have effectively equipped participants with practical upcycling knowledge and significantly boosted their confidence in customising outdated garments. Through hands-on practice, the participants have applied various upcycling methods and used their creativity to turn old garments into stylish new pieces. In other words, participants have gained valuable skills in restyling old clothes.

研究结果表明，四次工作坊有效地为参与者提供了实用的升级改造知识，并显著增强了他们定制过时服装的信心。通过实践，参与者运用了各种升级改造方法，并发挥创意将旧服装变成时尚新装。换言之，参与者获得了改造旧衣服的宝贵技能。

5. Conclusion

The findings indicate that the project has been largely successful in enhancing teenagers' refashioning skills and raising their awareness of sustainable fashion. While similar workshops should continue to be offered, there is some room for improvement to make the project more effective.

研究结果表明，该项目在提升青少年的旧衣改造技能及提高他们对可持续时尚的认识方面取得了很大成功。虽然应继续举办类似工作坊，但仍有一些改进空间，使项目更加有效。

6. Recommendations

In view of the positive findings, it is recommended that similar upcycling workshops be offered for different skill levels so that more teenagers will benefit, while previous participants will have the opportunity to advance their creativity and design capabilities.

鉴于正面的研究结果，建议为不同技能水平的青少年举办类似的升级改造工作坊，使更多青少年受益，同时让以往的参与者有机会提升创意和设计能力。

At the same time, it is advisable that revision materials, such as sewing tutorial videos, be provided so that participants can review at their own pace. To support continued learning, a resource bank of old clothes for upcycling could be established to enable easier access to materials for practice and experimentation.

与此同时，建议提供复习材料，如缝纫教学视频，以便参与者按自己的进度复习。为支持持续学习，可建立一个用于升级改造的旧衣资源库，方便获取练习和实验的材料。

Step 4: Writing Supplementary Information (Unit 5)

第四步：撰写补充资料（单元五）

For writing Supplementary information, identify the possible concern and solution and a concluding remark on your handwritten notes.

撰写补充资料时，请从手写笔记中识别可能的关注事项、解决方案及结语。
  • 1. Acknowledging Concern: We are aware that / We acknowledge / We understand that there might be concerns about ... [concern]
  • 1. 承认关注事项：我们明白 / 我们理解可能会有……的顾虑
  • 2. Proposing Solution: However, we will ensure that... / However, please rest assured that we will... [solution]
  • 2. 提出解决方案：然而，我们将确保…… / 请放心，我们将……
  • 3. Concluding Remark: We are confident that the project / [verb-ing phrase], which [project aim], will [summary of benefits / positive outcome].

Example 1 — Esports Tournament (Technical Server Lag):

We are aware that there might be concerns about technical issues, such as server lag during the tournament. However, please rest assured that we will secure high-performance servers and conduct pre-event stress tests so that a smooth experience for all participants will be guaranteed.

范例一 — 电竞比赛（服务器延迟技术问题）：
我们明白可能会有技术问题的顾虑，例如比赛期间的服务器延迟。请放心，我们将确保使用高性能服务器，并在活动前进行压力测试，以保证所有参与者享有流畅的体验。

We are confident that organising the esports tournament, which showcases the outstanding performance of local gamers, will foster a positive perception of competitive gaming among the public, gaining more support from society.

我们坚信，举办是次电竞比赛，展示本地电竞选手的出色表现，将促进公众对竞技游戏的正面看法，赢得社会更多支持。

Example 2 — EV Charging Station Network (Equipment Safety):

We understand that there might be concerns about the safety of the charging stations. However, we will ensure regular check-ups and maintenance of the charging equipment so that the risk of malfunctions can be minimized.

范例二 — 电动车充电站网络（设备安全）：
我们理解可能会对充电站的安全性有所顾虑。然而，我们将确保定期检查及保养充电设备，以尽量减低故障风险。

We are confident that installing the EV charging station network, which promotes sustainable transportation and a greener future, will have a significant and far-reaching impact on environmental conservation, promoting the adoption of EVs in the community.

我们坚信，安装电动车充电网络，推动可持续交通及更绿色的未来，将对环保产生深远影响，促进社区采用电动车。

Example 3 — Neon Installation Exhibition (Electrical Safety):

We acknowledge that there might be concerns about the safety of neon installations. However, we are committed to ensuring that all artworks comply with electrical safety standards and undergo professional inspection before being displayed in order to minimise risks.

范例三 — 霓虹灯装置展览（电力安全）：
我们承认可能会对霓虹灯装置的安全性有所顾虑。然而，我们致力于确保所有艺术品符合电力安全标准，并在展出前进行专业检查，以尽量减低风险。

We are confident that the neon exhibition, which supports local creative talent, will generate meaningful cultural impact and inspire interest in preserving the city's design legacy.

我们坚信，支持本地创意人才的霓虹灯展览，将产生有意义的文化影响，并激发人们保存城市设计遗产的兴趣。

Professional Workplace Communication: Proposal and Report Writing (LAN4103)

专业职场沟通：提案及报告写作（LAN4103）

Sample Mini-research Proposal on the Launch of Disposable Paper Water Bottle for Water Manufacturers

为瓶装水厂商推出一次性纸水瓶的研究提案范例

1. Background

Plastic bottles are a major source of environmental pollution. According to the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), "Plastic waste has more than doubled, from 156 Mt in 2000 to 353 Mt in 2019" (OECD, 2022, p. 14), and the majority comes from packaging, including single-use plastic bottles (OECD, 2022). Apart from environmental concerns, microplastics could also pose major health risks, such as cancers (Kumar et al., 2022). To reduce plastic bottle consumption, a product design studio has launched a disposable paper bottle designed for water manufacturers.

塑料瓶是环境污染的主要来源。根据经济合作与发展组织（OECD）的数据，「塑料废物从2000年的1.56亿吨增加到2019年的3.53亿吨，增长了一倍多」（OECD, 2022, p. 14），其中大部分来自包装，包括一次性塑料瓶（OECD, 2022）。除了环境问题外，微塑料也可能带来重大健康风险，如癌症（Kumar et al., 2022）。为减少塑料瓶的消耗，一家产品设计工作室推出了一款专为瓶装水厂商设计的一次性纸水瓶。

2. Objective

This mini-research aims to find out the views and attitudes of water manufacturers and consumers towards the paper bottle design. The findings will offer a reference for other beverage companies to consider adopting sustainable packaging.

本迷你研究旨在了解瓶装水厂商及消费者对纸水瓶设计的看法和态度。研究结果将为其他饮料公司考虑采用可持续包装提供参考。

3. Methodology

The mini-research will be conducted between 15–30 September 202-. An online questionnaire will be sent to around 20 local water manufacturers using the paper bottle packaging. About 50 consumers will also be invited to complete an online questionnaire. With prior approval from some convenience stores, I will visit them at agreed times and share with consumers a QR code linking to an online questionnaire.

迷你研究将于202-年9月15日至30日期间进行。一份网上问卷将发送给约20家使用纸水瓶包装的本地瓶装水厂商。亦将邀请约50名消费者填写网上问卷。在获得部分便利店事先批准后，我将按时到访并向消费者分享链接至网上问卷的二维码。
